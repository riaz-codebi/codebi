1. About this policy

CodeBI Solutions operates codebisolutions.com and the CodeBI Service Portal. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you visit our website, contact us, or use the portal. If your organization has a separate service or data-processing agreement with CodeBI, that agreement also applies to information handled for those services.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: your name, email address, identity-provider account identifier, verification status, and profile information, such as a profile picture, that Google or Microsoft shares during sign-in. Authentication services also process the session credentials needed to keep you signed in.
  • Company and contact information: information you or an authorized administrator provides, including company name, industry, website, business address, contact details, phone number, and timezone.
  • Portal records: your client association, role, account status, sign-in history, and records of administrative changes. We also process information you submit through available service features or send us while discussing your project.
  • Technical information: request timestamps, IP addresses, browser information, and error or security logs that our hosting and authentication services may record to operate and protect the website.

3. Google and Microsoft sign-in

We use Google and Microsoft sign-in to verify your identity, match you to your CodeBI account, and provide access to your organization's portal. Our Google sign-in requests basic identity permissions: OpenID, email, and profile. Microsoft sign-in similarly provides identity and email information needed for authentication. CodeBI does not receive your Google or Microsoft password.

Signing in does not authorize CodeBI to read your Gmail, Google Drive, Outlook messages, calendars, contacts, or Microsoft 365 documents. Those permissions are not part of the website's sign-in feature.

We use Google user data only for the disclosed account and service purposes. We do not sell it, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. CodeBI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, manage accounts and client roles, provide requested services, maintain accurate company records, communicate about your account or project, respond to support requests, troubleshoot problems, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Account and audit records help us investigate access problems and protect client data.

5. Service providers and sharing

We share information with service providers as needed to operate CodeBI: Supabase provides authentication, database, and configured storage services; DigitalOcean provides application hosting; and Google or Microsoft processes sign-in through the provider you choose. These providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy notices.

When you use address search, the search text and selected location identifier are sent to Google Maps Platform to return and fill address information, including timezone. Manual address entry is also available.

Authorized CodeBI administrators and your organization's authorized portal administrators can access information appropriate to their roles. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service, investigate misuse, or protect people's rights and safety. We do not sell personal information.

6. Storage, security, and international processing

Account and portal information is stored in our configured databases and hosting systems. We use HTTPS, authentication, role-based access controls, and protected server-side session storage to help safeguard information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Our providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. Applicable contractual and legal requirements govern processing performed for your organization.

7. Cookies and sessions

The portal uses essential cookies and session information to complete sign-in, keep you authenticated, and protect form submissions. Google and Microsoft may use their own cookies during authentication. Blocking essential cookies can prevent sign-in or portal features from working.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, administer accounts, maintain necessary business and security records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal or contractual obligations. Deactivating portal access does not automatically erase historical records.

You can request account closure or deletion of personal information by emailing contact@codebisolutions.com. We may verify your identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or contractual reasons; where applicable, we will explain any limits on your request.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to access or correct your personal information, request deletion, or raise questions about our handling of it. Your available rights depend on applicable law and your relationship with your organization. For records your organization controls, we may coordinate the request with its administrator.

You can revoke Google's access through your Google Account connections, or manage Microsoft permissions through your Microsoft account or organization. Revoking provider access does not itself delete records already held by CodeBI. Contact us separately if you want those records reviewed for deletion.

10. Children and policy updates

Our website and portal are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through our services.

We may update this policy as our services change. The date above identifies the latest version. If we introduce a new use of Google user data requiring additional disclosure or consent, we will provide that disclosure and obtain the required consent before that use.
